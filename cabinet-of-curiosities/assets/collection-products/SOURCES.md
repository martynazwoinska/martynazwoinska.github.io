These files are used only by the two Browse the collection lists. They do not replace the photographed board, object masks or detail-dialog previews.

## Zotter

Credit: © Zotter Chocolate.

Reuse basis: Zotter's public [press-photo page](https://www.zotter.at/en/about-zotter/press-downloads/logo-photos) explicitly offers individual product photographs for download and identifies this copyright credit. This is an editorial, non-commercial collection index, not a merchandise use. This is not a Creative Commons licence.

The linked SharePoint download server returned 403; no restricted files were accessed. The same maker's publicly accessible product-page photographs were downloaded instead. Original downloaded JPEG bytes are retained without cropping, recolouring, resampling or re-encoding. CSS displays the complete images at 64 × 64 px using object-fit: contain.

Visual review confirmed the product identities and cacao percentages where present. These are current product photographs: packaging editions, language and package size can differ from the historical wrappers on the board. In particular, the Uganda photo is the current upright red wrapper, not the older horizontal design.

## Storm & Bille: user-selected local preview

The user explicitly selected https://stormochbille.se/products/chili-72. The page heading and the front-wrapper photo both say 70%, and the photographed wrapper matches the collected bar. The earlier statement that the current product was 72% was incorrect: the URL slug alone is not product evidence.

The maker's purchase terms at https://stormochbille.se/pages/kopvillkor were reviewed; they do not state an image-reuse licence. The photo was tested locally but is excluded from publication. This entry uses the user's own photographed wrapper.

## User-photo fallback

The user approved using her own photographs wherever web pictures need extra clearance or matching. The other 27 chocolate entries use display windows onto the approved photographed object layer, without editing that source file. A failed web-photo load uses the same fallback. Metadata, detail previews and board art remain unchanged.

Other makers' images have not been cleared for reuse. Pump Street's [terms](https://pumpstreetchocolate.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) restrict copying beyond personal use; obtain permission before adding its product photos. Do not treat an image being publicly visible as a licence.

Cocoa Runners was checked as an alternative: https://cocoarunners.com/terms-conditions/ section 2.2 does not grant image-reuse rights. Its photos have not been copied. Other matching difficulties are not doubts about the user's confirmed bar identities: the Friis-Holm link shows a mixed bag rather than individual mini wrappers; the Fjåk product URL returned 404; the Firetree link is a collection with a landscape cover, not a bar photograph.

Chof's public catalogue is accessible, but its terms (https://chof.nl/terms) do not provide a republication licence. No Chof photographs are included.
