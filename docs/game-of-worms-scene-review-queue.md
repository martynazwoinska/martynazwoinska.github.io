## How to keep the list useful

Small shared-map issue noted during Nambucca QA: at the normal narrow browser
width, a pointer click targeted at Nambucca selected nearby Queensland. Keyboard
selection reached the correct record. Recheck these two hit areas separately
from the scene artwork. No map change was made in this preview.

1. Open this checklist first. Inspect only the next selected scene and its
   relevant files. Do not tour the whole atlas again.
2. Record the review date, specific finding and decision. Move a scene out of
   the queue when it is reviewed, even if the decision is to keep it unchanged.
3. Keep "implemented locally", "approved", "committed", and "live verified"
   separate. Add a commit/reference when publication actually happens.
4. Reopen a closed scene only when Martyna reports a problem, a relevant file
   changes, or a shared rendering/interaction regression could affect it.
5. A shared-system change needs targeted regression checks on affected scenes,
   not a fresh judgement of every landscape and accessory.
6. Reconcile the inventory only when locations are added or removed. The exact
   species/location pairs in these tables must match the current catalogue once
   each, so the two Kauaʻi and two Réunion scenes cannot be confused.
