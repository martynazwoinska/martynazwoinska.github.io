paired wiggle and smooth return. There is no autonomous repeat.

- Replaced ECA789's cacao-key xylophones with one shared bicycle and cacao cracker/winnower.

## Visual refinement

The first drawing was not treated as final. The refinement pass replaced solid
wheel interiors with open spokes, moved the drive belt behind the frame, placed
the scoop in front of the body with hands above its handle and rim, and moved
the larger worm away from the scoop so pouring reads separately from its face.
The final rig was raised clear of the location label. Output grains distinguish
nib chunks from thinner shell fragments.
