## Scope

- Replaced ECA789's cacao-key xylophones with one shared bicycle and cacao cracker/winnower.
- Preserved the approved background, collection record, Tiny surprise, petal trumpets and raindrop harps.
- The male turns the pedals with two hands. The larger worm pours roasted beans from a scoop.
- A closed chain, rear wheel, belt, roller and blower communicate the mechanism. Nibs and shells enter separate collectors.
- An 8.25-second batch has a smooth start, visible separation, coast and return. A second activation or Escape cancels. Home empties the collectors. Dragging or resizing cancels before editing the object.
- Size survives activation and cancellation. Other visible props are temporarily hidden during work and restored afterwards.
- The reduced-motion path uses a short static result and no audio.
- Two CC0 recordings provide bicycle/chain and bean-rattle foley, not synthetic musical cues. Their source files and hashes are documented in the audio source list.

## Visual refinement

The first drawing was not treated as final. The refinement pass replaced solid
wheel interiors with open spokes, moved the drive belt behind the frame, placed
the scoop in front of the body with hands above its handle and rim, and moved
the larger worm away from the scoop so pouring reads separately from its face.
The final rig was raised clear of the location label. Output grains distinguish
nib chunks from thinner shell fragments.
