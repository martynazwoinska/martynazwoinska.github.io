# Ahmedabad AF16 location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This dossier is the evidence and review contract for the Ahmedabad landscape and the three *Caenorhabditis briggsae* AF16 accessory pairs.

## Evidence boundaries

### AF16 collection record

AF16 is a wild *C. briggsae* isolate collected from soil in Ahmedabad, India. The available strain record does not identify a street, garden, riverbank, neighbourhood, collection date or finer microhabitat.

Sources:

- [Caenorhabditis Genetics Center AF16 strain record](https://cgc.umn.edu/strain/AF16)
- [CaeNDR AF16 isotype record](https://caendr.org/isotype/AF16/)

The dry soil patch in the illustration is therefore the direct collection cue. The historic lane and river are wider Ahmedabad context, not a reconstruction of the sampling site.

### Wider Ahmedabad setting

Ahmedabad was founded on the eastern bank of the Sabarmati. Its historic walled city is characterised by densely packed traditional houses organised into pol streets and inner khadki entrances. UNESCO records carved domestic architecture, public wells and characteristic bird-feeding structures among this urban fabric. Ahmedabad also has a semi-arid climate.

Sources:

- [UNESCO Urban Heritage Atlas: Historic City of Ahmedabad](https://whc.unesco.org/en/urban-heritage-atlas/ahmedabad/)
- [UNESCO Historic City of Ahmadabad nomination dossier](https://whc.unesco.org/document/155827)
- [Ahmedabad District history, Government of Gujarat](https://ahmedabad.nic.in/history/)
- [Ahmedabad district environment report, Government of Gujarat](https://ahmedabad.gujarat.gov.in/assets/downloads/DSRAhmedabad2022.pdf)

## Landscape composition contract

The illustration links the soil record to Ahmedabad through a shaded pol lane. It is a representative city composition rather than a claimed view from the AF16 sampling point.

### Foreground: collection material

- A modest patch of dry reddish-brown soil remains visible beneath and around the worms.
- Stone thresholds and packed lane surfaces frame the patch without turning it into a farm or desert.
- No fruit, compost, forest litter or invented sampling equipment appears in the landscape.

### Middle ground: pol lane

- Closely packed lime-plastered houses form a strong shaded perspective.
- Carved wooden brackets, projecting balconies, lattice screens and deep thresholds provide specific architectural texture.
- The two sides differ in colour, projection and carving so the lane does not read as a mirrored corridor.
- One slender chabutro bird-feeding tower is the principal vertical landmark.

### Background: city geography

- A narrow pale-blue Sabarmati glimpse closes the lane.
- The river remains secondary and does not become a modern landscaped promenade.
- No modern skyline, palace, broad boulevard, generic domes or unrelated national landmark appears.

The locked silhouette is a deep shaded lane between unequal carved façades, punctured by one slender chabutro and a narrow river opening.

## Accessory design contract

The accessories are playful designs derived from shade architecture, Ahmedabad’s kite craft, the Sabarmati and the documented soil collection. They do not claim to be authentic dress.

### Shade visor

- **Hermaphrodite:** a broad carved-teak jharokha visor with a scalloped shade edge, pierced side panels and a curved fitted band.
- **Male:** a smaller asymmetric jaali visor with a diamond lattice, indigo inset and separate side hinge.
- The two designs differ in silhouette and construction, and neither covers the eyes.

### Current-streamer wand

- **Hermaphrodite:** a large patang-style charkhi reel with carved rims, visible wound line and one long Sabarmati-coloured current ribbon.
- **Male:** a compact twin-spindle reel with two shorter forked streamers and a different hand grip.
- Both objects suggest Ahmedabad’s kite-making culture while retaining the approved current-streamer concept.

Sources:

- [Kite making, Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Government of India](https://handicrafts.nic.in/crafts/All_Crafts/Craft_Categories/Paper/Paper_Crafts/Kite_making/KiteMakingWebPage.html)
- [Ahmedabad Kite Festival, Gujarat Tourism](https://ikf.gujarattourism.com/about.html)

### Soil sieve

- **Hermaphrodite:** a round brass-and-wood field sieve with a fine square mesh, two handles, a shallow receiving tray and visible soil fractions.
- **Male:** a crescent hand-scoop sieve with radial perforations, one wrapped handle and a small separate sample vial.
- The pair differs in working action and outline rather than scale alone.

## Quality and interaction acceptance

- The painted background equals or exceeds the Bristol, Edinburgh and Ishigaki finish.
- The pol lane and chabutro are recognisable at thumbnail size.
- Soil remains the only claimed collection substrate.
- All six accessory copies use dedicated SVG geometry and differ between hermaphrodite and male.
- All three pairs remain legible together and do not hide either face.
- Each object moves independently by pointer and keyboard; Home resets only the focused copy.
- Visual checks cover 360 px, 768 px, 1024 px and wide desktop.
- Browser checks report no horizontal overflow, missing assets or console errors.
