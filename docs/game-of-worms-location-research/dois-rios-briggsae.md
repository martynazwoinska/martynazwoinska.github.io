# Dois Rios, Ilha Grande *Caenorhabditis briggsae* EG5612 location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Exact record

The CaeNDR reference-isotype page and its embedded strain records specify:

- species and reference strain: *C. briggsae*, EG5612;
- isotype members: EG5612 and EG5614;
- sampling date: 29 April 2009;
- coordinates: -23.18, -44.19, with the note giving
  -23.18234, -44.19456;
- locality: near Dois Rios, Ilha Grande, Brazil;
- elevation: 3 m;
- landscape: forest;
- reference substrate: rotten jackfruit;
- paired strain substrate: rotten red flower;
- sampled and isolated by M. Ailion;
- many animals of various stages emerged, but the reference strain was
  started from a single L4;
- the two samples travelled in the same bag, so the strains may not be
  independent and may have come from the same fruit;
- the reference did not form cross-progeny with N2 males but did with AF16
  males;
- environmental measurements: ND.

## Evidence boundary

The locality, coordinates, elevation, date, forest, jackfruit substrate,
paired red-flower record, shared-bag caveat, single-L4 founding and test-cross
notes are evidence. A cove, mangrove, island view, beach, water, property,
weather and exact collection-patch view are unreported.

The old Angra cove scene was geographically broad and invented mangrove roots,
water and islets. The replacement is a low Atlantic Forest floor near Dois
Rios with the documented rotten jackfruit.

### Shared sample bag

Primary: a large transparent zip specimen bag divided between a jackfruit cue
and a red flower cue, with a prominent `SAME BAG` label.

Companion: a narrower bag in the same upright orientation with independently
proportioned specimen cues.

### Test-cross plates

Primary: two overlapping culture plates in a laboratory carrier, with the N2
cross marked negative, the AF16 cross marked positive and a separate `L4`
founder tab.

Companion: a compact carrier in the same orientation with smaller independently
proportioned plates. The marks summarise the recorded test-cross outcomes.
