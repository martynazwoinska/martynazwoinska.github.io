# Ishigaki location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This dossier is the evidence and review contract for the Ishigaki landscape and the three *Caenorhabditis inopinata* accessory pairs.

## Evidence boundaries

### Type-strain collection record

The wild-type strain NK74SC was isolated from fresh syconia of one *Ficus septica* tree on Ishigaki Island:

- coordinates: 24°24′38.06″N, 124°11′06.81″E;
- elevation: 71 m;
- collection date: 6 June 2013;
- material: about 30 fresh figs, collected manually;
- condition: attached, fresh figs rather than rotting fruit;
- processing: figs were cut in half and placed individually on agar plates.

Subsequent field work found *C. inopinata* predominantly inside fresh, pollinated *F. septica* figs. Reproducing worms dominated earlier fig phases, while dauer larvae occurred in later figs with emerging pollinating-wasp progeny. The association with *Ceratosolen* pollinating wasps supports dispersal between figs.

Sources:

- [Kanzaki et al., 2018, Biology and genome of a newly discovered sibling species of *Caenorhabditis elegans*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-05712-5)
- [Woodruff and Phillips, 2018, Field studies reveal a close relative of *C. elegans* thrives in fresh figs](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6102938/)
- [NCBI BioProject PRJDB5687](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/382947)

The exact record establishes one fig tree and does not establish a mangrove, beach or reef collection site.

### Wider Ishigaki setting

Iriomote-Ishigaki National Park protects a regional sequence of subtropical evergreen forest, mangrove and tidal-flat systems, and coral-reef waters. The Sekisei Reef lies between Ishigaki and Iriomote, while mangroves occur around river mouths and bays. These are valid wider island cues, not the worm’s recorded substrate.

Sources:

- [Iriomote-Ishigaki National Park, Ministry of the Environment](https://www.env.go.jp/en/nature/nps/park/iriomote/index.html)
- [Adjacent waters of Iriomotejima and Ishigakijima Islands, Ministry of the Environment](https://www.env.go.jp/en/nature/biodic/kaiyo-hozen/kaiiki/engan/15101.html)

## Accessory evidence and design contract

The three accessories translate the fig–fig-wasp ecology into playful objects. They do not imply that the worms wear these structures in nature.

### Fig UV visors

- **Female:** a broad translucent aqua shield with a berry brow, gold lower rim, visible side hinges and a cut-fig medallion with a small leaf.
- **Male:** a separately proportioned compact shield with lighter hardware and its own smaller fig medallion.
- Both follow the head angle, retain a transparent view of the face and stay clear of the eyes.

### Sample pannier

- **Female:** a fitted oval woven side pannier with a curved handle, raised hinged lid, two whole fresh fig samples and one visibly cut fig.
- **Male:** two unequal sample cups on a light padded yoke, with one whole fig, one cut fig, a separate collection tag and no backpack outline.
- The pair differs in silhouette and construction rather than scale alone.

### Fig-wasp wings

- **Female:** a long translucent forewing pair with smaller hindwings, clear branching veins, layered gold wing roots and a fitted curved crossed harness.
- **Male:** a shorter fan-shaped pair with different venation, smaller root plates and a compact crossed harness.
- Wing edges remain visible over both light worms and the painted vegetation, while faces remain unobstructed.

## Quality and interaction acceptance

- The painted background equals or exceeds the Bristol and Edinburgh finish.
- The attached fresh fig is more prominent than mangroves or reef.
- All six accessory copies use dedicated SVG geometry and differ between female and male.
- The fascinator never disappears or clips when focused, touched or dragged.
- All three pairs remain legible together and do not hide either face.
- Each of the six objects moves independently by pointer and keyboard; Home resets only the focused copy.
- Visual checks cover 360 px, 768 px, 1024 px and wide desktop.
