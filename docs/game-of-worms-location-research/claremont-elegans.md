# Claremont *Caenorhabditis elegans* ECA250 location research and art brief

## Status

Active replacement for Auckland ECA36. The public marker uses the current CaeNDR reference strain ECA250 while the collection story is explicitly described as a lineage-level record.

## Evidence boundary

- CaeNDR lists ECA250 as the reference for an isotype that includes CB3199, DR1345, ECA249 and ECA250, with PA3 as an alternative name.
- The CGC CB4857 record traces this lineage to a decaying mushroom collected during rain in Claremont, California, in November 1972.
- The exact property, mushroom species, coordinates and collection-day viewpoint were not recorded.
- Public coordinates use a rounded Claremont city marker, not a claimed collection point.

Sources:

- [CaeNDR ECA250 isotype record](https://caendr.org/isotype/ECA250/)
- [CGC CB4857 strain record](https://cgc.umn.edu/strain/CB4857)

## Approved environment

The production background is an elevated three-quarter view across a rain-darkened terracotta courtyard. A small decaying mushroom cluster and broken clay pot sit in deep litter at lower left. Rectangular wet tiles, an off-centre stucco wall, citrus canopy, bougainvillea and a narrow foothill opening create a recognisably different composition from the game's low forest and garden-floor scenes.

Regional elements do not claim to reconstruct the unknown property. The scene must not acquire a generic lawn, street, broad skyline or exact-site label.

## Accessory set

1. **Bookworm books** — a large open illustrated volume and a separately constructed smaller hardback, each with a clearly legible `BOOKWORM` title treatment, visible covers, page blocks, spines and bookmarks. The literal bookworm joke refers to Claremont's identity as a college town.
2. **California lemonade** — a faceted handled pitcher and a distinct handled tumbler, built from visible glass rims, liquid boundaries, ice, lemon wheels, straws, highlights and condensation. They make the set feel like one warm garden reading day rather than another collection-equipment display.
3. **Sunny reading glasses** — wide berry-and-violet frames for the hermaphrodite and smaller rounder indigo-and-aqua frames for the male. Each version is separately fitted to its worm's face, with lenses, rims, bridge, temples and glints kept clear of the eyes.
