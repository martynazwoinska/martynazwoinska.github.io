# Nambucca Heads *Caenorhabditis briggsae* QG2814 location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Exact record

The CaeNDR reference-isotype page and its embedded strain records specify:

- species and reference strain: *C. briggsae*, QG2814;
- isotype members: QG2814 and QG2815;
- sampling date: 25 March 2017;
- coordinates: -30.6445167, 153.0090333;
- elevation: 21 m;
- landscape: urban garden;
- locality: rainforest garden behind Marcel Towers, Nambucca Heads, New South
  Wales, Australia;
- substrate: rotting flowers on the ground;
- sampled and isolated by M. Rockman;
- the two strains came from the same flower sample;
- the reference was derived from a single L4 picked from the first of two
  plates on 30 March 2017;
- the paired strain began from a single young adult from the second plate;
- the note records *C. briggsae* identification by 18S sequencing;
- environmental measurements: ND.

## Evidence boundary

The locality, coordinates, elevation, date, urban rainforest-garden setting,
ground-flower substrate, same-sample relationship, two-plate isolation,
single-L4 founder and 18S identification note are evidence. The flower
species, property access, weather, estuary view, neighbouring plant species
and exact collection-patch view are unreported.

The old estuary scene invented a water bend, banksia and eucalyptus. The
replacement uses the documented rainforest garden behind a tower, with the
architecture kept subordinate.
