# Poovar, Kerala *Caenorhabditis briggsae* JU1337 location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Exact record

The CaeNDR reference-isotype page records:

- species, isotype and reference strain: *C. briggsae*, JU1337;
- alternative name: 1K1;
- sampling date: 16 December 2007;
- coordinates: 8.32, 77.075;
- elevation: 34 m;
- landscape: agricultural land;
- substrate: rotting nut, pod, seed or fruit;
- sampled by M.-A. Félix;
- substrate temperature, ambient temperature and humidity: ND;
- a singleton isotype.

Cutter et al. (2010) identify the location as Poovar, Kerala and describe the
collection material more broadly as rotting fruit, leaf litter, bark and soil.
The CaeNDR category is used for the Game's exact substrate statement.

## Evidence boundary

The coordinate, elevation, date, agricultural landscape, broad rotting
plant-material substrate, collector, singleton-isotype status and alternative
name are evidence. The plant species, crop, property, weather, irrigation
system, water body and collection-patch view are unreported.

The present generic paddy bands, irrigation channel and coconut emphasis are
too specific. The replacement may show a low humid agricultural edge with
anonymous mixed cultivation and lateritic soil, but no named crop. A very
distant low palm rhythm may supply regional context without claiming that the
sample came from coconut.

## Environment contract

Use a preserved painted 3:2 source PNG and optimized WebP. Low ground-level
view, with live SVG worms and accessories above it.

- Foreground: warm damp lateritic soil, leaf litter, bark fragments and one
  collapsed anonymous rotting plant-material cluster.
- Middle: asymmetric low banks and mixed anonymous cultivated vegetation,
  broken by a narrow working gap rather than a picturesque path.
- Background: humid green depth and a subordinate, distant palm rhythm.
- Keep the middle clear for the worms.
- No paddy geometry, irrigation canal, river, backwater, beach, named crop,
  house, temple, people or invented fruit identity.
