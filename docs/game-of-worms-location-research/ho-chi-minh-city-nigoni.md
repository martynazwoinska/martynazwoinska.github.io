## Purpose

This dossier defines the evidence boundary and illustration contract for the
Ho Chi Minh City · JU4356 environment and its three paired accessories.

## Exact collection record

- Species recorded: *Caenorhabditis nigoni*
- Strain: JU4356
- Former name: HCM1
- Genotype: wild isolate
- Line state: isofemale
- Landscape: urban garden
- Substrate: rotting fruit on the ground
- Substrate comment: carambola
- Collection date: 30 August 2022
- Coordinates: 10.7742239, 106.6939439
- Sampled by: Mirko Francesconi
- Isolated by: Marie-Anne Félix
- Species-identification method field: test cross
- Record note: ITS2 pointed to *C. nigoni* and crosses with JU1325 worked
  in both directions.

The record does not report elevation, exact source tree, reliable property
name, collection weather, temperature, humidity, associated organism,
population size, males observed or a field photograph.

The place-like text in the note is corrupted. It must not be normalised into
a named park, institution or property without confirmation from the source
lab.

## Evidence boundary

The carambola, its contact with the ground, the date, coordinates, urban-garden
landscape and strain history are exact record evidence.

A generic mature urban canopy, anonymous path edge and very narrow city-edge
glimpse are regional context. They must not be presented as a reconstruction
of the collection property.

Carambola morphology may follow authoritative botanical descriptions: a
drooping branched tree, pinnate leaves, small purple flowers and a strongly
ribbed fruit. The scene must not claim that a visible tree was the source of
the collected fruit.

## Later sequencing record

NCBI assembly GCA_977007565.1 is a later JU4356 genome assembly. Its linked
BioSample reports Hong Kong and 17 October 2024. Those fields describe later
sequencing-sample provenance and must not replace the documented wild
collection in Ho Chi Minh City on 30 August 2022.

### JU4356 ITS2 ribbon reader

diagnostic result.

unnumbered registration marks and a hinged uncertainty shutter.

Companion: a tall three-window spool tower with vertical ribbon, offset side
dial and a separate lower catch reel.

Do not draw bases, nucleotide letters, sequence alignments, match percentages
or a definitive-result lamp.
