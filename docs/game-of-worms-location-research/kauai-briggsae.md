# Kauaʻi *Caenorhabditis briggsae* QG130 location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Exact record

The CaeNDR reference-isotype page records:

- species, isotype and reference strain: *C. briggsae*, QG130;
- sampling date: 2 August 2009;
- coordinates: 22.2202, -159.5829;
- elevation: 11 m;
- landscape: forest;
- substrate: rotting nut, pod, seed or fruit;
- sampled by M. Rockman;
- substrate temperature, ambient temperature and humidity: ND;
- three strains in the isotype: QG128, QG129 and QG130.

## Evidence boundary

The coordinate, elevation, date, forest landscape, broad rotting
plant-material category, sampler and three-strain isotype are evidence. The
fruit identity, exact collection-patch view, property, weather, water,
mountain, coast and neighbouring plants are unreported.

The former north-shore valley scene invented taro, pandanus, a watercourse,
waterfall, ridge and sea glimpse. The replacement is an anonymous,
low-elevation humid forest floor with no named valley or landmark.

## Environment contract

Use a preserved painted 3:2 source PNG and optimized WebP. Keep live SVG worms
and accessories above it.

- Foreground: dark volcanic soil, wet litter and one anonymous collapsed
  brown-gold plant-material cluster.
- Middle: a broad, calm oval forest-floor clearing framed by unequal roots.
- Background: layered glossy understory and filtered humid light.
- Silhouette lock: one broken upright trunk on the far left opposed by one
  low arching root from the right.
- No waterfall, stream, sea, ridge, taro, pandanus, beach, landmark or named
  fruit.

## Accessory contract

### Kukui-shell glow carts

Primary: a deep ribbed nutshell cart with a gold luminous chamber, long curved
root handle, two unequal vine wheels and separate hubs.

Companion: a compact, more upright shell cart with an aqua light chamber,
different ribs and independently proportioned wheels. It is not a scaled clone.

The shell is a playful wider-island forest reference, not an identification of
the anonymous collected plant material.

### Root-loop carousel rides

Primary: a crooked root mast supports an asymmetric berry canopy and four
differently coloured hanging leaf-pod seats above a rooted base.

Companion: a shorter, differently bent mast supports two unequal pod seats and
a compact separate base.

### Three-worm ribbon hoops

Primary: three large interlocking gold, aqua and berry hoops use different
angles, three junction knots and a dark rooted stand.

Companion: three tighter hoops form a more vertical arrangement with different
overlap, proportions, knots and base. The trio playfully echoes the three
closely related QG128–QG130 isolates; it is not collection equipment.
