# Praslin *C. nigoni* location research and art brief

Last updated: 2026-08-24

> **Current production accessory contract:** `giant-tortoise shell costumes`, `black-parrot carnival caps`, and `Moutya dance-bell bands`. They are playful Praslin- and Seychelles-linked fantasy rather than collection equipment; the exact YR106 substrate and collection point remain unknown.

This dossier is the evidence and review contract for the *Caenorhabditis nigoni* Praslin landscape and its three accessory pairs. It follows the evidence-led, location-by-location Game of Worms illustration workflow.

## Evidence boundaries

### YR106 island-level record

The public record for **YR106** establishes only an island-level wild-isolate provenance:

- species: *Caenorhabditis nigoni*;
- strain: YR106;
- genotype: wild isolate;
- other name: PR3;
- locality: Praslin, Seychelles;
- published coordinates: only coarse latitude -4.3, longitude 55.7;
- exact collection point, date, elevation, substrate, microhabitat, collector and isolator: unreported.

A recent study calls YR106 an isofemale line sequenced with ONT GridION; its accompanying table lists source lab YR and sequencing by Zhao Lab. The NCBI YR106-1 BioSample lists Hong Kong and 19 October 2024, which conflicts with the authoritative Praslin wild-isolate provenance and is treated as downstream culture or sequencing metadata rather than original collection evidence.

The alias `PR3` does not establish a place. Unrelated uses of the code in other studies are not evidence about YR106.

Primary sources:

- [Félix Lab YR106 record](https://www.justbio.com/tools/worms/details.php?strain_id=333579)
- [Andersen Lab *C. nigoni* metadata table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AndersenLab/CB_PopGen_HDR_MS/refs/heads/main/supplementary_data/SD3_c_nigoni_information.tsv)
- [Moya et al. *C. nigoni* genomic preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2025.12.08.693002)
- [NCBI YR106-1 assembly](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/GCA_977007545.1/)

The current four-decimal Game point remains only a representative Praslin marker. It must not be printed or described as a collection coordinate.

### Correct regional setting

The current Vallée de Mai title and sea opening imply an unsupported collection site and sightline. Praslin's granitic palm forest supplies suitable island context, but the scene must say that this is regional rather than sampled habitat.

Regional sources document pink-grey granite, humid forest, watershed drainage, coco-de-mer monitoring and black-parrot research. They do not establish YR106's substrate or exact site.

Reference sources:

## Landscape composition contract

The scene is an enclosed, continuous granitic palm-forest context rather than a tourist shoreline or claimed Vallée de Mai collection.

### Foreground: explicitly regional forest floor

- Intact palm litter, exposed pink-grey granite and a narrow boulder-choked drainage thread form the closest layer.
- No invented sample substrate, fruit, flower or collection container appears.
- An off-centre cleft boulder and diagonal water thread lead into the forest rather than forming another circular pond.

### Middle ground: palm-forest vault

- One concave forest slope carries unequal coco-de-mer trunks, a stilt palm, *Pandanus* and overlapping giant fan leaves.
- Fan rosettes differ in size, angle and construction and overlap into one spatial canopy.
- The artwork does not show collecting, cutting or carrying protected palm parts.

### Background: Praslin lock silhouette

- A closed granitic ridge and layered humid canopy leave only a narrow light opening.
- The locked thumbnail silhouette is an off-centre cleft boulder and diagonal drainage thread beneath two unequal giant fan rosettes forming a vaulted canopy.
- There is no sea, beach, coastal panorama or claim that the scene is YR106's exact habitat.

## Accessory evidence and design contract

The accessories are deliberately imaginative regional costumes. They do not represent the collection method, the unknown substrate, authentic local dress or wildlife handling.

### Giant-tortoise shell costumes

- **Primary:** a broad domed shell fitted to the larger worm's curve, with a thick gold rim, seven contained scute panels, belly harness and two fastening loops.
- **Companion:** a smaller, higher-domed shell with five differently arranged panels and a shorter independent harness.
- The shell construction provides the identity; neither version turns the worm into a realistic tortoise or implies wildlife contact.

### Black-parrot carnival caps

- **Primary:** a deep dark cap with curved berry band, double feather-shaped fabric crest, eye detail and a small gold beak pin.
- **Companion:** a shallower cap with a shorter split crest and a differently curved brim.
- The caps are stylised carnival fantasy inspired by Praslin's black-parrot identity. They do not use real feathers, obscure either face or imply capture.

### Moutya dance-bell bands

- **Primary:** a broad woven indigo band fitted across the larger worm, with four constructed bells, visible clappers and tied ends.
- **Companion:** a narrower curved band with four smaller bells in a different arrangement and its own ties.
- The bells are an imaginative rhythm cue for the game. They are not presented as a documented part of traditional Moutya dress.

## Cultural and ecological safeguards

- Never claim YR106 came from Vallée de Mai or use UNESCO property coordinates as its collection point.
- Do not show collecting, cutting or carrying coco-de-mer nuts or fronds or present them as drums, bowls, helmets or souvenirs.
- Do not disclose or invent protected tree or black-parrot nest locations.
- Clearly label the costumes as playful regional fantasy; do not present them as authentic Seychellois dress, traditional Moutya costume, collection equipment or institutional symbols.

## Quality benchmark

The revised preceding locations are the minimum quality floor, not templates to repeat. Praslin must exceed them through a coherent enclosed forest, protected-species-aware details, integrated mechanisms, two genuinely different drawings per concept and a readable combined composition.
