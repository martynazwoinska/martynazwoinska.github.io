# Six-location accessory refinement

2026-09-05. Scope: 18 paired accessory concepts at Nambucca Heads QG2814,
Ho Chi Minh City JU4356, Saint-Benoît JU1373, Araucanía, Dois Rios EG5612 and
Kauaʻi QG130. No landscape, collection record, public label or scientific
paragraph changed. These are playful props, not reconstructions of collection equipment.

## Construction and refinement

- Nambucca: open screw press versus closed strap press, layered glass dishes,
  portrait study cards versus a landscape fold-out.
- Ho Chi Minh City: ridged carambola and cut slices on round versus rectangular
  trays, folded cards, sliding-jaw calipers with locking knobs.
- Saint-Benoît: layered torch-ginger bracts on separate tray constructions,
  round versus shield enamel badges fitted to the bodies, two rain-gauge forms.
- Araucanía: metal bucket rims and handles, visible compost and trowels,
  open ash collectors with rulers, glass dishes with unobstructed direction arrows.
- Dois Rios: cut rind and individual jackfruit arils, zip bag versus gathered
  pouch, separately arranged dishes with lifted lids.
- Kauaʻi: shaped nutshell carts with spoked wheels, root-mast carousels with
  canopy-connected suspension, overlapping hoops with edge depth and root bases.

The critical review changed the companion trays to rectangular constructions,
rebuilt the small DNA card as a fold-out, moved the badges onto the bodies and
extended carousel suspension to the canopy. The first draft was not the final render.

## Structural references

- [Kew plant preservation](https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/preserving-plants-for-the-future):
  paper layers and compression inform the presses.
- [National Weather Service rain gauge](https://www.weather.gov/iwx/coop_8inch):
  funnel, measuring tube and outer collector inform the main rain-gauge construction.
- [Haglöf calipers](https://haglofsweden.com/products/calipers/):
  graduated beam, fixed jaw and sliding jaw.
- [USGS ash sample collection](https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/volcanic_ash/collecting_samples.html):
  an open collection area informs the ash collectors, distinct from funnel rain gauges.

These references guide object structure. They do not establish which apparatus
was used to collect any featured worm.

## Validation

All six production scenes have uncropped full-page proofs at 360, 768, 1024
and 1440 px in the Game's fixed light palette, plus scene-detail captures.
All 36 pieces remain within their scenes. Catalogue and pair-geometry audits
pass. Pointer dragging, keyboard movement, independent paired movement and
Home reset are tested at 360 and 1440 px. Reduced motion is respected.
The Node regression test guards dispatch, distinct paired geometry and all
SVG gradient references. Browser proof files stay under ignored `tmp/`.
